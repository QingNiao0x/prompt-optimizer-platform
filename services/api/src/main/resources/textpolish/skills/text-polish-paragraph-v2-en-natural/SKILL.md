Paragraph editing example (natural personal blog, restructuring allowed)
Source
When I think about the occasion on which I took my first bicycle ride to work, the part that continues to stand out to me is a conversation at the traffic light. I had started down the wrong street and stopped at the light. Another rider asked whether I was looking for the riverside path. I said I was, and she pointed to the entrance behind the bakery.

I used that entrance again the next day. This time I got to work without taking a wrong turn.

Revision
On my first bicycle ride to work, I took a wrong turn and stopped at a traffic light. Another rider asked if I was looking for the riverside path. When I said yes, she pointed to the entrance behind the bakery. That's the conversation I remember most about that ride.

I took the same entrance the next day and got to work without a wrong turn.

Meaning check
The opening puts the reader at the traffic light, while the memory still belongs to the narrator. It preserves the wrong turn, the rider's question and directions, the entrance behind the bakery, and the following day's ride. The new order uses only details given by the narrator.
