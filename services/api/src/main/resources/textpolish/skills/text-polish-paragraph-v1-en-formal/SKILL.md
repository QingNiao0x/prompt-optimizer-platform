Editing example (formal)
Source: In the course of the inspection, it was established that three of the sixteen cabinets were missing labels. The cabinets may return to use once the labels are in place.
Weak revision: During the inspection, it was found that three of the sixteen cabinets lacked labels. The cabinets may be used again after the labels are in place.
Effective revision: Three of the sixteen cabinets inspected had missing labels. They may return to use once labelled.
Overreach: Three cabinets failed the safety inspection. Missing labels do not establish a safety finding.
