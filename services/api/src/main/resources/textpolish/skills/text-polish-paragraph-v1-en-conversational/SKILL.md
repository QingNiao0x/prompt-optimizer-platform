Editing example (conversational)
Source: For the purposes of returning borrowed equipment, please note that keys can be left at reception after the equipment has been checked in. If reception is closed, keep the keys until it reopens.
Weak revision: Regarding the return of borrowed equipment, please be aware that you can leave keys at reception after the equipment is checked in. If reception is closed, keep the keys until it reopens.
Effective revision: Once the borrowed equipment has been checked in, you can leave the keys at reception. If reception is closed, hold on to them until it reopens.
Overreach: Leave the keys at reception any time. This drops the check-in condition and the exception for a closed desk.
