FORMAL: Make the finding, responsible party and conditions easy to find. Use precise written language and retain terms needed for accuracy.
NATURAL: Let the content guide transitions and rhythm. Preserve the author's point of view, attitude and existing details.
CONVERSATIONAL: Explain terms and relationships in words the intended reader understands. Keep necessary terms, obligations and level of certainty. Keep the author's actual relationship to the reader.

LIGHT allows full sentence rewrites, splits and combinations inside existing paragraphs, retaining paragraph order and format. RESTRUCTURE allows paragraphs to be split, combined or reordered when useful, preserving chronology, causal direction and which conditions govern each action. Clear passages may stay as written.

These three revisions illustrate the same source. They retain the same observations, conditions and requirements.

Source: In connection with the trial recording arrangements, staff on duty must note the filter pressure difference before closing; it measures how much the air pressure differs on the two sides of the filter. A reading above the stated limit requires staff on duty to notify the supervisor. The trial does not require staff on duty to replace the filter.

FORMAL: During the trial, staff on duty must record the filter pressure difference before closing. This measures the difference in air pressure on the two sides of the filter. Staff on duty must notify the supervisor if the reading exceeds the stated limit. The trial does not require them to replace the filter.

NATURAL: During the trial, staff on duty need to note the filter pressure difference before closing. The reading shows how much the air pressure differs on the two sides of the filter. If it goes above the stated limit, they need to let the supervisor know. Replacing the filter is outside what's required of them in this trial.

CONVERSATIONAL: During the trial, staff on duty need to note one reading before closing: the filter pressure difference. That's how much the air pressure differs from one side of the filter to the other. If the reading is above the stated limit, staff on duty must tell the supervisor. The trial doesn't ask them to replace the filter.
