Paragraph editing example (formal team email, restructuring allowed)
Source
I am writing in connection with preparations for our annual stock count and would appreciate it if each supervisor could send me the final inventory totals by noon on Friday. If a count is still in progress, an estimate is fine by the same deadline, as long as it is marked provisional.

I will use the figures to schedule the checks. Please leave loaned items out of both the final totals and the estimates.

Revision
Could each supervisor send me their final totals for the annual stock count by noon on Friday? If your count is still in progress, you can send an estimate by the same deadline. Please mark it provisional.

I'll use the figures to schedule the checks. Please leave loaned items out of both the final totals and the estimates.

Meaning check
The request states who sends what, to whom, and by when. The unfinished count allows a labelled estimate at the same deadline. The second paragraph preserves both the purpose of the figures and the exclusion that applies to both types of submission.
