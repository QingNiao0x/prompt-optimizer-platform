Editing example (natural)
Source: While I was sorting through the desk drawers, I came across the ticket I had kept from my first visit to the coast. I had forgotten the date, though I remembered how long the train ride felt.
Weak revision: As I was going through the desk drawers, I found the ticket I'd saved from my first trip to the coast. I couldn't remember the date, but I recalled how long the train ride seemed.
Effective revision: In a desk drawer I found the ticket I'd kept from my first trip to the coast. I'd forgotten the date. I still remembered how long the train ride felt.
Overreach: I remembered the hours spent watching fields go by. The duration and the view from the train are new details.
